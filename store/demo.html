Lemon garlic spaghetti

25 minutesServes 4★★★★★ 4.8 (312 reviews)
Jump to the method
🍋🍝

A bright, fast dinner from the pantry: spaghetti tossed with garlic softened in olive oil, lemon zest, a splash of the pasta water and plenty of parmesan. It is on the table before the salad is dressed.

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 5 tbsp olive oil and 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 2 unwaxed lemons, zest and juice
  • 60 g parmesan, finely grated
  • A handful of parsley, chopped

Method

  1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until just shy of al dente.
  2. Warm the oil and garlic gently until the garlic turns pale gold.
  3. Keep a mug of pasta water, drain, and toss everything together off the heat.
  4. Loosen with the pasta water until glossy, then add the lemon juice to taste.
